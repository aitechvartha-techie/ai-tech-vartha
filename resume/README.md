## What it does
- **Interviews you first**, one short question at a time (Malayalam or English). Paste your old resume or a
  job ad and it only asks about what's missing.
- **6 designs**: three ATS-safe for job portals (clean, elegant, compact) and three designer looks for sending
  straight to a person (sidebar, timeline, monogram). Your colour, A4 or Letter, 1 or 2 pages, margins, section
  order (fresher or experienced), renamed or hidden sections, optional round photo. Or build your own design (mix header, headings, skills style, timeline, fonts), or show it a resume design you like and it matches it.
- **Matches the job**: paste the job ad and it shows which of the ad's main needs your resume covers and what's
  missing, using the ad's exact words only where they're true for you.
- **Sounds like you, not like AI**: no "results-driven, passionate, spearheaded…". Every bullet says what you did
  and what changed, with real numbers it asks you for. Summary, cover letter and LinkedIn text also go through a
  humanizer pass based on **blader's Humanizer** (26 patterns of AI writing, from Wikipedia's "Signs of AI writing").
- **Readable by recruiter software (ATS)**: single column, real text, standard headings.
- **Review loop**: it grades your resume for the job (out of 100), asks you up to 3 questions about what's
  missing, rewrites, and grades again until it's strong (85+). It only adds what you confirm.
- **Extras**: a matching cover letter, a LinkedIn headline and About section, and likely interview questions with tips.
- **Never invents** jobs, degrees, certificates or numbers.

## Setup (സെറ്റപ്പ്)

### claude.ai or the Claude desktop app
1. ഈ folder-ലെ **resume-builder.zip** download ചെയ്യൂ. (Download resume-builder.zip from this folder.)
2. claude.ai-ൽ **Settings → Capabilities**-ൽ **Code execution and file creation** ON ആക്കൂ.
3. **Customize → Skills** (or Settings → Skills) → **+** → **Upload a skill** → ആ ZIP തിരഞ്ഞെടുക്കൂ.
4. പുതിയ chat-ൽ: `Make my resume` എന്ന് എഴുതൂ. Job ad ഉണ്ടെങ്കിൽ അതും paste ചെയ്യൂ.

> Custom skills may not be available on every plan. If you don't see Skills in your settings, your plan may not
> include it yet.

### Claude Code
Unzip into `~/.claude/skills/resume-builder/` and ask: "Make my resume".

## Saving the files
- **Computer**: click the PDF and download it.
- **Phone app**: tap the file → Share → Save to Files / Drive.
- The **.docx** opens in Word or Google Docs (Drive → Upload → Open with Google Docs) for later edits.

## Tips
- Before you start, think of 2–3 numbers from each job: how many customers, bills, students, patients, sales, hours saved.
- Make one version per type of job. Paste each job ad for a matched version.
- Don't post your resume publicly with your phone number on it.
