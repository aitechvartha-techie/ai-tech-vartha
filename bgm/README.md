Mass movie BGM maker that runs in your browser: hero entry, villain, fight, kuthu, dappankuthu, slow-mo swag,
sad hero and heist vibes. Press Play, then change the tempo, key, hook, drums, sound, structure and arrangement
while it plays, or tap Surprise me for a new one every time. A little robot dances to it on the dance floor.

(Pick a vibe and press Play; change anything while it plays.)

- Every sound is made live in the browser (Web Audio). No samples, no sign-up, nothing to install.
- Patterns are original; nothing is copied from film songs.
- Works on phone and laptop. On a phone, turn off silent mode to hear it.
